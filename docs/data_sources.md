# Data provenance

The runnable repository uses **no external dataset** and redistributes no third-party raw data. All identifiers, applications, policy decisions, performance, recoveries, and audit groups are generated by `credit_risk.simulation` with a fixed random seed. They are fictional and must not be represented as real customers or empirical lending results.

Potential public datasets are intentionally not bundled because suitability, target semantics, redistribution rights, and temporal fields require dataset-specific review. Before adding one, record provider, canonical source URL, exact license/terms, fields, coverage, transformations, limitations, and redistribution decision here. Public availability alone is not permission to redistribute.

Synthetic audit group labels are segregated from `MODEL_FEATURES` and exist only to exercise fairness monitoring. No SSN, account number, address, or real credit report is generated or stored.
